👉 ШАГ 1: Создаем ёлочные игрушки из фото (5 минут на фото)

1. Откройте бота Syntx (https://t.me/syntxaibot?start=aff_244030591) в Telegram

2. Перейдите: Главное меню → 🎨 Дизайн с ИИ → 🍌 Nano Banana (https://t.me/syntxaibot?start=aff_244030591)  (Выбирайте модель PRO)

3. Загрузите фотографию близкого человека

4. Отправьте промпт:

Using the provided reference, transform the main subject into a hyper-realistic Christmas tree ornament in the same style as the example:
a small detailed figurine hanging on a golden thread from a pine branch.

Recreate the reference subject as a miniature ornament figurine with accurate proportions, textures, colors, clothing (if present), and facial features, but rendered in a solid, toy-like material (polymer, resin, or painted ceramic).
Ensure the surface looks slightly glossy and finely crafted, like a premium Christmas ornament.

Place the figurine hanging from a golden string attached to a natural pine branch.
Surround it with realistic Christmas tree needles and warm festive bokeh lights (gold, red, soft white).
Use shallow depth of field with the ornament in perfect focus and the background blurred.

Lighting: warm, soft, diffused, with natural reflections on the figurine's surface.
Camera: full-frame DSLR, 85mm lens, f/1.8, ultra-sharp subject, creamy bokeh.
Composition: centered, straight-on angle, full view of the ornament from top to bottom.

The final result must look exactly like a handcrafted Christmas tree ornament version of the reference subject.

Повторите для каждой фотографии (мама, папа, дети, бабушка и т.д.)

👉 ШАГ 2: Оживите игрушки (5 минут)

1. В том же боте Syntx (https://t.me/syntxaibot?start=aff_244030591) переходите: Главное меню → 🎥 видео будущего → Seedance (https://t.me/syntxaibot?start=aff_244030591)

2. Загрузите созданные ёлочные игрушки и отправьте промпт:

Animate the scene of a Christmas tree decorated with realistic miniature figurine ornaments. Each figurine subtly comes to life one by one. The man figurine gently tilts his head and slightly moves his eyebrows. The boy figurine makes a small nod. The girl figurine blinks softly and adjusts her scarf. The seated figurines make a small hand and foot movement as if waking up. Keep all motions very subtle, warm, and magical. Preserve the original look of the ornaments and the Christmas tree. Add soft glowing holiday lights and a warm, cinematic atmosphere. No exaggeration — only gentle, lifelike micro-movements. Make the whole animation feel cozy, magical, and realistic

4. Сохраните каждое видео (по 5 секунд)

👉 ШАГ 3: Монтируем финальный ролик (10 минут)

1. Откройте приложение CapCut
2. Загрузите все анимированные ролики
3. Добавьте новогоднюю музыку на фон
4. Выберете "Монтаж с ИИ"
5. В поле с промптом напишите:

Сделай плавное соединение кадров под музыку, чтобы получился полноценный видеоряд с плавными переходами и эффектами

6. Сохраните готовое видео
