3D PVC Patch 

Для генерации я использовал Nano Banana 2. Но вы можете взять любой удобный инструмент.

Так же можете встроить свой логотип: просто загружаете его как референс, и модель повторяет форму, стиль и детали.

👩‍🎨 Вот базовый промпт, с которого можно начать:
Generate a hyper-realistic image of a custom-shaped 3D PVC patch where the patch outline perfectly follows the [logo] silhouette, maintaining brand-accurate colors. The patch is sewn onto [color] fabric with visible, precise stitching. The patch should have raised, dimensional details with a [surface] PVC finish, visible stitching around the edges, and realistic texture. The fabric should show natural weave patterns, slight wrinkles, and appropriate lighting that highlights the 3D depth of the patch. Focus on commercial product photography quality with sharp details, professional studio lighting, and a clean composition that emphasizes both the patch's dimensionality and the fabric's material properties.
⚡️Экспериментируйте с материалами (leather, denim, tactical fabric), освещением и типом поверхности (matte / glossy / rubber).
